Student • Engineer • Builder

Hello, I'm
Minjun Kim.

I am a student interested in engineering, computer science, electronics, and the systems that connect technology with the real world. I learn by building, experimenting, and taking things apart to understand how they work.

I have always been drawn to things from the past. It is not simply their age or the sense of warmth they carry. To me, the objects left behind by each era are traces of human history—evidence of how people thought, what they valued, the problems they faced, and the technologies they developed to solve them. That is why I do more than collect old things. I want to understand the people behind them, the ideas that shaped them, and the technology that made them possible. I believe understanding the past is more than preserving history. By looking closely at the technologies and values of earlier generations, we may discover things that are easy to lose when we focus only on technological progress, and perhaps approach the future more thoughtfully. I want to understand the technologies of the past, restore and reinterpret them with the tools of today, and discover new possibilities within them. From exploring old radios, audio equipment, and records to using signal processing and programming to bring aspects of the past back to life, the things I do ultimately lead to one question: What can we learn from the past, and how can we carry it forward into the future?

Curiosity is where engineering begins.

Elementary School — Learning by Playing
Explored programming through Scratch and Minecraft Education, experimenting with code to create and change virtual worlds. I also invited neighborhood friends to my home and taught them what I had learned.

Middle School — Mathematics & Community
Earned 1st place in a mathematics competition and founded a student association at Tilden Middle School to support newly transferred students. Built a website to help students connect and share information.

9th Grade — First Software Projects
Took an introductory computer science course and began building software projects, including a traditional Korean card game.

10th Grade — Code Meets the Real World
Studied AP Computer Science A, AP Economics, AP Calculus BC, and AP Physics C while developing Economia, an economics simulation game that combined programming, mathematics, and economic systems.

11th Grade — Computing & Signals
Expanded into college-level study through dual enrollment, taking courses in data structures, algorithms, and linear algebra. I also began exploring digital signal processing through a historical audio restoration project.

High School — Radio & Morse
Explored Morse code, amateur radio, electronics, and communications, eventually earning an Amateur Radio Technician license.

Community & Connection
Supported ESOL and newly transferred students through peer mentoring and leadership, and helped organize International Night as a representative of the Korean community.

2026 — Connecting Past & Future
Bringing together electronics, computer science, signal processing, and historical technology to understand, restore, reinterpret, and build upon what came before.
