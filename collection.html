04 / Collection

Things I collect.

A collection of vintage machines, recordings, cameras, and other objects that reflect my interest in technology, engineering, and the history of everyday life.

Collection 01
Mechanical Sound

Hand-crank Phonographs

Mechanical phonographs that operate without electricity. Each machine is powered by winding a spring by hand, which drives the turntable and reproduces sound mechanically.

Collection 02
Recorded Sound

SP Records

SP records and shellac discs from the early history of commercial recorded sound, collected for their recordings, labels, artwork, and historical character.

Collection 03
Photography

Vintage Film Cameras

Mechanical film cameras from different eras, collected for their lenses, mechanisms, industrial design, and the history of photography.

Collection 04
Mechanical Writing

Typewriters

Vintage typewriters and mechanical writing machines, collected for their engineering, craftsmanship, and tactile design.

Collection 05
Other

Other Collections

Other vintage objects, machines, and pieces of technology that reflect an interest in how people designed, built, and used technology in earlier eras.
