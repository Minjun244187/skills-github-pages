04 / Collection

Things I collect.

Vintage phonographs, typewriters, recordings, cameras, and other pieces of old technology collected over the years. Found through auctions, thrift stores, and antique shops, each piece is carefully researched, along with the history of its time, then repaired and restored. What began as a hobby has become a passion for understanding the value of these pieces and preserving them.

Collection 01
Mechanical Phonographs

Phonographs & Gramophones

A collection of Edison phonographs and cylinder records produced from 1896 onward, gramophones, a German turntable from the 1960s, and parts used for cleaning and restoration. Collected since 2021 (9th grade).
